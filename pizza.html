How it works

  1. On your phone open the watch side and copy the son's link (it carries a private alert channel).
  2. Send that link to your son. He opens it at school, taps Set school to my current spot, then Start tracking before leaving for lunch.
  3. When he leaves school you get a “left school” note; when he reaches Big Taste Pizza your phone sounds, vibrates and pops up 🍕.
  4. Keep the track page open on his phone until he arrives (it keeps the screen awake).

Alerts travel through ntfy.sh on a random private topic — only someone with the link can see them. No accounts, no app install needed (the ntfy app is optional for extra push backup).
