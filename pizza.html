How it works

  1. Pair the two sides — pick one direction: on the track phone tap Copy watch link and open it on the watcher's device, or on the watch side tap Copy son's link and send it to your son. Either way both sides land on the same private channel (shown as 📻 Channel on the track side).
  2. Your son opens the track side — the school (St. Marguerite d'Youville) is already pinned — taps Check GPS to confirm a sharp fix, then Start tracking before leaving for lunch.
  3. When he leaves the school property you get a “left school” note; if he strays more than ~150 m off the usual walking route you get an “off course” warning; when he reaches Big Taste Pizza your phone sounds, vibrates and pops up 🍕.
  4. Keep the track page open on his phone until he arrives (it keeps the screen awake).

Alerts travel through ntfy.sh on a random private channel — only someone with the link can see them. No accounts, no app install needed. While walking, his position sends about once a minute (the free alert service caps daily messages, so the app rations them — a whole trip uses ~25).
